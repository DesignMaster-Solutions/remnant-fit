# Planning notes: Remnant Fit

Why this idea: we build Stone Project (Remnant Finder), a marketplace where countertop fabricators list the stone remnants left over from big jobs. The question every homeowner asks about a remnant is "will it fit my vanity?", and today they answer it with a tape measure and a photo. Glasses can put the slab on the cabinet at 1:1.

Decisions taken while planning with the plan-lens skill:
- One spatial moment: the slab on the vanity top. Kitchens were dropped because a real-size kitchen slab never fits in the 37 x 32 degree view.
- The cabinet is a stand-in with a "Set top here" button, because the preview can't detect a real surface.
- Rotate is a button, not a two-hand twist, so it can be tried with the mouse and tested with LEAF.
- No black granite in the sample data: the display is additive and black would vanish.
